The Problem

AI writers are fundamentally broken.

Every tool on the market has the same fatal flaws. Here is why none of them actually work at scale.

Hallucinated numbers

Made-up tax rates. Payment tables that add up to 130%. Confident citations of data that does not exist. One fact-check and the whole article falls apart.

Robotic copy

Every article opens with the same formula. Google and ZeroGPT flag it above 80%. Your readers can smell it before they finish the first paragraph.

Manual labor

Generate. Copy. Paste. Format. Find an image. Upload. Write meta tags. Publish. The AI part is 10% of the actual work.

No source of truth

Ask it about current tax rates and it confidently cites 2022 data. There is no verified knowledge base anchoring the output to reality.
